05Method

Tool-assisted.
Signed off by a person.

  • A lead is not a findingA name match or a label points you somewhere; you decide what it means.
  • Chain facts apart from claimsA transfer on the ledger, a list entry and an explorer's label are kept as what they are.
  • Gaps stay visibleOnly the latest transfers, labels only where public, a list not yet imported: the page says so.
  • Every number has a source and a dateIncluding the checksum of the list version you screened against.
How it is built

One graph, two engines, the same queries

The public pages run on Relate, the embedded graph database by Neo4j, right in your browser: nothing to install, nothing sent to us. Your investigations are kept in your browser today; for teams they will be kept on our servers, on Neo4j Community, separated per organisation. Both speak openCypher, so a question asked here is the same question asked there.
